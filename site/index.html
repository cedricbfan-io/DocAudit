How it works

Five steps from a photo to a ledger row — the system never replaces a number on its own.

  1. 1Upload

    A receipt photo, or one of the demo receipts.

  2. 2Read

    PaddleOCR finds every text line and its position.

  3. 3Check

    Rules test totals, duplicates and prices, showing the calculation.

  4. 4Review

    Accept or reject each row and the TOTAL.

  5. 5Export

    CSV + JSON — only when every entry is accepted.

What it checks

A small set of well-defined checks, each explained by a rule and a highlighted image region.

Incorrect total

Items + tax vs the printed TOTAL, and every line's qty × unit price.

Duplicate line

The same item printed twice on one receipt, even with small OCR misreads.

Unusual price

A unit price far from reference prices (robust median test).

A warning is a signal to review, not proof of fraud. When the app cannot read enough of a receipt it says “not enough data to check the total” instead of guessing.

You decide what gets exported

One rejected row — or a rejected TOTAL — blocks the whole receipt.

Privacy

Receipts are processed only to show you the result; uploads and exported files are deleted automatically within about an hour. Please do not upload receipts that show card numbers or other personal data.
